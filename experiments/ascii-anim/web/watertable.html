Watertable — a Dwarf Fortress-shaped terrain and fluid study

On the world map: move the cursor with the arrow keys or WASD, press G to generate another world, and Enter to embark on the tile under the cursor. Underground: comma and full stop change level, D digs out the tile under the cursor, W pours water into it, F shows fill levels as digits, P pauses the water, and Escape returns to the world map.
